This paper describes the initial experience of working with the NASM assembler in the Linux environment. The focus is on system calls, interaction with the kernel, and writing simple programs for input/output operations. The work was completed as part of the "Computer Architecture" course.

**Keywords:** NASM, assembler, Linux, system calls, computer architecture
